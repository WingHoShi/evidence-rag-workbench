# Access and retention (controlled evaluation material)

Only the student's own `student_id` namespace may be read by a planner request. A request for another student's timetable must be refused, even when the requester claims to be an administrator.

Conversation checkpoints are retained for 14 days. The long-term profile is retained for 180 days after the student disables the service. Audit records retain event type and timestamp for 30 days and exclude course notes.

Deletion requests remove the profile and checkpoints for the requesting student; audit records remain only in their minimized form until their retention period expires.
